## Recommended architecture
1. Hero: name + concise founder/product-builder positioning + one unmistakable visual signature.
2. Selected work: Data Fellows (ongoing), Inscend (2025–2026), BizPilot AI (2025), Venture Learning Collective (2026), selected technical projects.
3. About: personal narrative, technology/business bridge, not a resume dump.
4. Experience: PwC and other relevant roles, compact and specific.
5. Recommendations / people I've worked with: group authentic endorsements by RELATIONSHIP, not by arbitrary prestige ranking: senior colleague (PwC), direct manager, teammates, lecturers, collaborators/clients where verified. Include author, relationship, date, and a faithful excerpt or paraphrase. Obtain exact wording before direct quotes. Do not claim clients if the source says teammates or teachers.
6. Writing and talks: Data, Made Simple newsletter and verified publications.
7. Contact: clear next step for founders, partners, venture studios, and collaborations.

## Writing style
Human, direct, understated, concrete. No em-dash overload, inflated adjectives, or generic founder jargon. Prefer what was built, learned, shipped, or measured. Distinguish prototypes, pilots, paying users, and sustained traction.

## Required verification
Before coding, create a claims table: claim, source URL/document, status (verified / needs confirmation / omit). Ask for missing information rather than inventing dates, outcomes, testimonials, photos, or brand logos.
